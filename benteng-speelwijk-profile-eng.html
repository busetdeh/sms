Safety & Conservation Commitment

Heritage Preservation &
Safety Protocols

Key Commitment
Zero
Damage
  • Race flight lines strictly avoid fragile historical stone structures.
  • Gates and obstacles are self-standing, ensuring zero physical penetration into heritage ground or masonry.
  • Direct on-site coordination with BPK Banten (Heritage Preservation Office) and Disparpora.
  • 3-meter high safety netting securing the spectator and pilot paddock zones.
Pre-Event

Joint Feasibility Survey.
Technical and safety inspection prior to any track assembly.

During Event

Dedicated Safety & Cleanliness Crew.
Continuous monitoring to ensure structural safety and site preservation.

Post-Event

Complete Site Restoration.
Thorough site cleanup and sterilization completed within 24 hours.
